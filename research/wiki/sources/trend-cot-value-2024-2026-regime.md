# Regime record 2024-2026: trend, COT, and value in the OOS window

External corroboration that the gate's OOS window (~2024-06 → 2026-07, the last 20%
of a 2600-day panel) was a hostile regime for exactly the factor families we
rejected:

**Trend / tsmom.** Professional trend followers — the SG Trend Index constituents,
i.e., the best-capitalized implementations of this signal class — were flat in 2024
(+2.4% SG CTA), then lost ~9% in the first four months of 2025 with a -12.5%
rolling 12-month stretch, hit by the Aug-2024 equity reversal and the Apr-2025
vol spike/reversal. 2026 recovered on energy trends but "with multiple trend
reversals" (Kpler Q1-2026 note; NHX April 2026). A tsmom OOS sign flip in this
window is what the industry record predicts for the signal class, not evidence of
a broken construction per se.

**COT positioning.** Academic record is that COT-signal predictability is
time-unstable even in-sample-friendly studies (ScienceDirect: commercial net vs
S&P returns "not stable across time"; position-based sentiment signals
insignificant on average). A train t=6.4 → OOS flip is consistent with a signal
class whose documented property is instability, compounded by our N≈small
cross-section (29-39% flip probability from sampling noise alone — see
[[questions/research-oos-gate-design]]).

**Equity value.** 2024-2025 was an extreme megacap-AI growth rally (S&P >20%
years driven by AI names; largest-20 concentration at records; banks at ~40%
P/E discount vs 20y avg ~19%). Long-E/P-short-expensive was a documented headwind
trade through most of the window, with tentative broadening in 2026. Regime
dependence of value in this window is corroborated; note our train span
(~2016-2024) also contains value's "lost decade," which explains a *negative*
train t for E/P.
